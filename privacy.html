Privacy at a Glance

CruiseLog is a personal logbook. Your entries stay on your device, the app contains no advertising and no tracking, and it does not share any data with third parties.

Data Processed by the App

You enter your logbook data (titles, notes, dates) yourself. Location data is only collected if you grant permission – the app uses your location exclusively while in use and attaches it to your entries. You can take photos with the camera or select them from your photo library.

Purpose and Legal Basis

The data is used exclusively for using the app: keeping your personal logbook, displaying entries on a map, and generating PDF exports. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for location data it is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Storage and Synchronization

Your data is stored locally on your device. With iCloud sync enabled, it is additionally stored encrypted in your private CloudKit database with Apple and synchronized across your devices.

PDF Export and Sharing

PDF files are generated locally on your device. Your data leaves the device only when you actively share it – for example via email, AirDrop, or the Files app.

No Third-Party Sharing, No Tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics tools, and no tracking technologies. No personal data is transmitted to or sold to third parties.

Your Rights

You can view, edit, or delete your data in the app at any time. For questions about data protection, contact us via the email address above.

Deletion

Deleted entries and trips are permanently removed. Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data from your device.
